# Icons

- `desertbird.svg`: the DesertBird mark, a flat desert owl in a cowboy hat on an 80 by 80 view box that starts at x 8 and y -1. The square crop centers the owl and clips the thin brim tips. It has no poncho, so the shape stays readable at 16 and 48 pixels. The orange, red and tan come from the DesertFox logo palette. The hat is a lighter brown (`#8a5632`) so it shows on a dark toolbar, and the pupils keep the darker brown (`#3b2417`). It is the toolbar button icon and the manifest icon at 48 and 96.
- `desertbird-128.png`: the mark rendered at 128 px with `rsvg-convert -w 128 -h 128`. It is the manifest icon at 128 and the listing icon.
